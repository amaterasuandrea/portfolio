ABOUT

Andrea Graffeo

Video Editor & Filmmaker

I am a visual storyteller obsessed with the rhythm of the cut. For me, editing isn't just about assembling footage—it's about finding the hidden tempo within the frames and shaping emotions through timing, color, and sound.

With a foundation in both the technical and artistic sides of post-production, I bridge the gap between creative vision and cinematic reality. Whether it's meticulous color grading to establish a mood, seamless VFX integration, or dynamic motion graphics, I treat every project as a unique canvas.

My approach is heavily inspired by analog techniques, bringing a raw, authentic texture to modern digital workflows. I strive to make the digital feel tangible.
